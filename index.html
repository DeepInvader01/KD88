How DKP is calculated

This page explains how DKP is calculated in KD3688 and why the system is more detailed than a simple kill-point ranking. The goal is a fair and transparent evaluation of wartime contribution, reward distribution, and Autarch distribution.

1) Basic DKP Calculation

DKP is based on kills and deads during wartime.

Action DKP Value
T4 Kills1 point
T5 Kills2 points
T4 Deads7.5 points
T5 Deads15 points

Total DKP is the sum of kill DKP and dead DKP.

2) Power-Based DKP Requirement

DKP requirements scale with starting power. The system is progressive, meaning each power bracket is calculated separately. Higher-power players still benefit from the lower brackets, similar to a tax system.

Power Bracket Multiplier
First 45M power0.5×
45M to 70M power0.75×
Above 70M power1×

These values may be adjusted in future KvKs if needed.

3) Minimum Dead Requirement

At least 20% of your DKP must come from deads. If this requirement is not met, your DKP can be reduced accordingly.

Dead DKP matters because it shows actual sacrifice, not only kill farming.

Example: if your deads are too low, your total DKP is capped based on your dead contribution. This helps prevent KP chasing and keeps the KvK burden fair.

4) Rally and Garrison Leads

Rally and garrison leads often have fewer open-field opportunities because they need to focus key marches, gear, and commander setups on kingdom objectives.

  • Rally leads can receive a DKP bonus.
  • Garrison leads can receive a higher DKP bonus.

This recognizes responsibility while keeping their evaluation fair.

5) External Activity

If a player is outside the kingdom during non-war periods, leeches Golden Chests elsewhere, or is less active in KD3688, their DKP requirement can be increased.

Being part of the kingdom means contributing to matchmaking and seed position. That comes with responsibility.

6) Main Principle

Equal rewards require equal contribution.

DKP reflects meaningful participation:

  • Kills matter.
  • Deads matter.
  • Activity matters.

The full DKP list is available in-game and on Discord.
